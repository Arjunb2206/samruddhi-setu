> 50kg Farmer Selling Policy

Understanding Direct Farmer Sales vs. Cooperative Society Aggregation

1. Volume > 50 kg (Dual-Option Selling)

When a farmer harvests more than 50 kg (e.g. 500 kg tomatoes, 20 quintals onions, or 200L milk), they have two authorized pathways:

  • Option A: Sell through Cooperative Society: The farmer routes produce via a nearby society. The society provides bulk grading, cold storage, refrigerated transport, and locks in wholesale contracts with luxury buyers.
  • Option B: Sell Directly (Farmer Direct): The farmer bypasses the society and lists directly on the Samruddhi Setu marketplace as an independent grower with direct farm-gate pickup.

2. Volume ≤ 50 kg (Direct D2C Mode)

Smaller household quantities are listed directly on the consumer marketplace for instant bike delivery without requiring cooperative consignment handling.
